# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Rising football players (grassroots to semi-professional, aged 16–30) seeking a verified path to professional recognition. Also: certified coaches and performance experts monetising their expertise; scouts and clubs sourcing talent through validated data; sponsors identifying rising stars early.

Context: players are ambitious and mobile-first, often accessing from pitches and changing rooms; experts are professional and time-conscious; scouts need data-dense but quickly scannable profiles. The platform spans 48 countries, so global reach with personal stakes is the constant tension to hold.

## Product Purpose

Outceedo is the global football talent ecosystem — a single platform where players build verified performance profiles, book elite expert assessments, and gain visibility with scouts and sponsors worldwide.

Success looks like: a player discovered through the platform landing a professional contract; an expert building a sustainable income stream from their knowledge; a scout identifying three club-ready prospects in a single session.

## Brand Personality

Elite, Relentless, Global. The energy of Nike's athlete campaigns combined with the credibility of a UEFA technical report. Confidence without arrogance. The platform that believed in you before the scouts did.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboard** (Intercom, HubSpot, Notion): corporate blue-and-white palette, clinical UI strip-mined of athletic soul, overly polished and emotionally sterile.
- **Traditional sports media** (BBC Sport, Sky Sports): TV-news layout aesthetic, editorial grid, reporting *on* football rather than feeling *part* of it. Stuffy, uninspiring, zero hunger.

## Design Principles

1. **Athletic architecture**: Every layout decision should feel like it was made on a pitch, not in a boardroom. Structural boldness over decorative flair.
2. **Data earns trust**: Numbers, credentials, and performance metrics must read instantly and credibly. The score on the card is as important as the hero headline.
3. **Global stage, local stakes**: The platform serves 48 countries but every player's journey is personal. Scale without erasing human ambition.
4. **Relentless momentum**: Motion, transitions, and interaction patterns should feel like forward progress — never idle, never purely decorative.
5. **One platform, two modes**: The public marketing shell and the signed-in product experience share one design language. A new user and a returning player should feel equally at home.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Custom cursor must degrade gracefully on touch devices. Reduced-motion media query is implemented; all animations must respect it. All interactive elements require visible focus states. Football is global — keep layout decisions RTL-aware for future i18n.
